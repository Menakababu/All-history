## The story

Amma asks "what do you want?" and each wish comes true as a tiny magical adventure. Kutty is shrunk to ant size by sparkles, then sleeps in a nest, sails a paper boat and rides a butterfly. The final wish, "a friend who always shows love", is answered by Poochaandi, who steps out as a gentle, loving friend.

## Hooks (so kids go crazy)

1. **"Where's Poochaandi?" game.** Poochaandi is hidden in every wish scene (S2–S5): his horn tip in the ant doorway, his peacock feather in the nest, his skull-headlight glow under the paper-boat bridge, his wheel sparkle behind a butterfly. Kids will rewatch to find him. Then he is revealed in S6.
2. **Magic shrink-sparkle** transition in every scene: a swirl of golden sparkles and "pop!".
3. **Soft "Bullet Bounce" (gentle version)** in the S6 reveal: REV-REV (tiny puffs of glitter), small WHEELIE, then a heart-sparkle point at Kutty. Kutty copies it, so kids can copy it too.
4. **Kutty's sprout** wiggles with joy at every wish.
5. **Payoff:** Poochaandi, once imposing, gives Kutty a flower and a hug-sized high-five, so the "friend" line lands emotionally.

## Flow-safety rules

- Start every prompt with: `Family-friendly, G-rated gentle fantasy cartoon.`
- The child is always safe, calm and smiling: tiny life jacket on the paper boat, a calm pond with lily pads, the butterfly flies gently at low height over flowers. No falling, no danger.
- Poochaandi is gentle in this song: soft smile, holding a flower. No threats, no weapons.
- No brand or studio names, no real people, no readable text.
- If Flow blocks a prompt, replace "flames" with "golden sparkles" and retry.
- Upload your Poochaandi image and ask for a **3D animated, friendly** version first (Step 1), and reuse it as the reference.

## Workflow in Flow

1. Make the 3 character sheets (Step 1). Reuse the Amma and Kutty sheets from the earlier file if you already have them.
2. Generate each scene's start image with the sheets attached as references.
3. Image to Video with that image as the first frame and the matching video prompt.
4. Veo can't lip-sync to your song. Prompts say "no music, no speech"; Amma's and Kutty's mouths move as if singing. Add a lip-sync tool for exact sync.
5. Flow's native lengths are 4/6/8 s. For 10 s clips, generate 8 s and extend, or slow it to about 80 %.
6. Each wish has a **combined clip (8 s)**: Amma asks, then the wish plays out. If the shrink transition looks odd, use the **alternate wish-world clip (10 s)** (Section "Alternates") for the wish part and a short Amma close-up for the question.
