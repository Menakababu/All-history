## Workflow in Flow

1. Make 3 character sheets first (Step 1). Poochaandi = your image.
2. Make every scene image with the character sheets attached as references (Flow "Ingredients"/reference images).
3. Then Image to Video using that scene image as the start frame, with the matching video prompt.
4. Veo cannot lip-sync to your song. Every video prompt says "no music, no speech" so the audio is clean. Mouths move as if talking and you lay the song over it in the editor (use a lip-sync tool if you want exact mouth sync).
5. Flow's native lengths are 4/6/8 s. For a "10 s" clip, generate 8 s and extend it (Extend) or slow it to ~80 %. 8 s clips need no extra step.

# Editing tips

- Place clips on the timeline using the window times in each heading, then slide by ear since the timing is approximate.
- If a block feels short, add B1–B4 or repeat the chorus clip (b) with a different crop.
- Because verse 5 and 6 share lyrics, S14–S16 and S17–S19 are deliberately different locations.
- Keep "Mudiyaadhu pongamma" lines on the Kutty-refusing clip (a) and the "venaam mummy" lines on the pleading clip (c).
