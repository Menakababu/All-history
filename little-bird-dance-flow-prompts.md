## Hooks (so kids go crazy)

1. **"Count along" moves:** the "one, two, three" flap, "hop hop hop" and "chirp chirp chirp" are made for kids to count and copy.
2. **Chiku the mascot** pops into every clip (hatching, riding on a head, copying the dance). Kids will look for him.
3. **Animals copy the kids** with a funny delay, a cute running gag in every verse: the frog hops too late, the hedgehog peck-rolls, the bunny does the flap with his ears.
4. **Rainbow-colored feathers:** wing feathers change color on each beat.
5. **Big payoff:** at the final "touch the sky", a rainbow appears and everyone does the big pose.

## Flow-safety rules

- Start every prompt with: `Family-friendly, G-rated cheerful children's cartoon.`
- The children never really leave the ground except small jumps. For "fly, fly, fly" they run with arms out and tiptoe-jump, and real birds, butterflies and a rainbow fly above. No heights, no balloons lifting kids, no danger.
- The costumes are full-body, modest and cute (hooded bird costume with a beak hood, wings on the arms and a tail).
- No brand or studio names, no real people, no readable text.
- If Flow blocks a prompt, remove any detail on "flying" and use "dancing on tiptoe with arms out".

## Workflow in Flow

1. Make the 5 character sheets (Step 1).
2. Generate each scene's start image with the sheets attached as references.
3. Image to Video with that image as the first frame and the matching video prompt.
4. Veo can't lip-sync to the song. Prompts say "no music, no speech" and the kids' mouths move as if singing. Dance timing is matched by describing counts ("on the 1-2-3 beats"), then you fine-tune in the editor.
5. Flow's native lengths are 4/6/8 s. For 10 s clips, generate 8 s and extend, or slow it to about 80 %.
