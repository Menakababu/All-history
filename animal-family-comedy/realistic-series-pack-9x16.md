# Animal Family Comedy – REALISTIC (live-action) Series Pack
## Real animals in human costumes · 9:16 Shorts · Google Flow (Character section + Omni model)

**Look:** photorealistic live-action cinema. Real animal heads, bodies, fur and skin, wearing real fabric clothes and living a human life. Not cartoon, not 3D animation, not illustration.
**Cast (same 4 characters, same story):** Tommy (pug, son), Rangasamy (bulldog, father), Gajalakshmi (elephant, mother), Mullai (elephant, daughter).
**Contents:** characters → Episode 01 (பொண்ணு பாக்குற படலம்) → Episode 02 (மாப்பிள்ளை விருந்து).

All files use the prefix **`R_`** so they never clash with the cartoon files.

Every prompt block starts with **PROMPT TYPE**, **ATTACH INGREDIENTS** (exact files, Omni allows 6–7) and ends with **SAVE AS**. Every video block also has a **WHO SPEAKS** line, and each line of dialogue inside the prompt is tagged `[SPEAKER: name]`.

### Rules for the realistic look
- **Style phrase used in every prompt:** photorealistic live-action cinematic film look, shot on a cinema camera, 35mm lens, shallow depth of field, natural lighting, real animal anatomy with real fur or skin texture, real fabric clothes, NOT cartoon, NOT 3D animation, NOT illustration.
- **Talking animals:** the animal's real mouth moves in sync with the Tamil words, with expressive eyes and brows, like a live-action talking-animal film.
- **Body language:** dogs sit and stand upright and use their paws. Elephants use their trunks to hold, lift and gesture. Do not give any animal human hands or a human face.
- **Scale:** Tommy is child-sized, Rangasamy is a small adult size, the elephants are clearly bigger, and Mullai is about twice Tommy's height. Elephants sit on a wide wooden divan, not a normal sofa.
- **Uncanny-valley fix:** keep clothing simple and fitted, avoid very large mouth movements, and stay in medium shots when a line is spoken.
- **9:16:** faces in the upper two thirds of the frame, bottom fifth and right edge clear for the Shorts buttons. The 6-angle body sheets stay 16:9 because they are reference images only.
