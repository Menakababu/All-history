# "பொண்ணு பாக்குற படலம்" – Animal Family Comedy
## Production pack v3 – 9:16 vertical for YouTube Shorts (Google Flow, Character section + Omni model)

**Locked:** 4 characters, 3 scenes, 3 scene images, 3 videos. Everything is **9:16**.
- Family 1 (groom's side): father + son (dogs)
- Family 2 (bride's side): mother + daughter (elephants)
- Animals in human costume, human activities, real animal bodies.

Every prompt block starts with **PROMPT TYPE** (IMAGE or VIDEO), **ATTACH INGREDIENTS** (exact files; Omni allows 6–7) and ends with **SAVE AS**.

**Shared style (already inside every prompt):** Stylized 3D animated feature-film look, Pixar-quality, expressive anthropomorphic animal characters who walk upright and behave like humans, true animal heads, fur or skin, snout or trunk, ears, tail, wearing traditional Tamil Nadu clothes, soft cinematic lighting, **9:16 vertical**.

### What changed for 9:16 / Shorts
- Frames are built **tall**: characters are stacked in depth (foreground / background) and not spread side by side. Faces sit in the **upper two thirds**.
- Keep the **bottom ~20% and the right edge** free of important faces or action. Shorts puts the title and buttons there.
- The **first second must carry the hook** (Scene 1 opens on the father's proud line).
- **Exception:** the 6-angle body sheets stay wide (16:9). They are reference images only and are never published, and six full-body views are unreadable in a vertical frame. Everything that ends up on screen is 9:16.
- Total runtime 28 s (10 + 8 + 10), well inside the Shorts limit.
