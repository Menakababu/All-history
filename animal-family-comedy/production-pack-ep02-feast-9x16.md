## PART 0 – Series guide (comedy + twist)

**Series formula (every episode):**
1. **Setup (Scene 1):** an ordinary Tamil family situation. Dad brags or someone makes a confident claim.
2. **Misdirection (Scene 2):** the other family reacts, and the audience guesses the wrong reason.
3. **Twist + callback (Scene 3):** the true reason shows up in the last 4 seconds, with a dad shout ("அடப்பாவி…") as the punchline.

**Running gags:** Tommy is lazy and wants to be carried. Mullai is much stronger than she looks. Rangasamy always learns the truth last. Gajalakshmi hides her giggle behind her saree pallu.
