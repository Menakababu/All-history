## Real animals in human costumes · 9:16 Shorts · Google Flow (Character section + Omni model)

**What changed:** the bride's family (mother + daughter) are now **real eastern grey kangaroos** instead of elephants. Tommy (pug) and Rangasamy (bulldog) are **unchanged**.
**New gag:** Mullai carries Tommy in her **pouch**.

**Reuse from the elephant set (do NOT regenerate):**
- `R_CH01_Tommy_Portrait.png`, `R_CH01_Tommy_BodySheet6.png`
- `R_CH02_Rangasamy_Portrait.png`, `R_CH02_Rangasamy_BodySheet6.png`

**New in this file:** 2 new Character cards (Gajalakshmi, Mullai), 2 portraits, 2 body sheets, then Episode 01 and Episode 02 scene images and videos re-written for kangaroos. New files use the prefix **`K_`**.

Every prompt block starts with **PROMPT TYPE**, **ATTACH INGREDIENTS** (exact files, Omni allows 6–7) and ends with **SAVE AS**. Every video block has a **WHO SPEAKS** line, and each line inside the prompt is tagged `[SPEAKER: name]`.

### Rules for the realistic look
- **Style phrase in every prompt:** photorealistic live-action cinematic film look, shot on a cinema camera, 35mm lens, shallow depth of field, natural lighting, real animal anatomy with real fur, real fabric clothes, NOT cartoon, NOT 3D animation.
- **Talking animals:** the real mouth moves in sync with the Tamil words, with expressive eyes and ears.
- **Body language:** the dogs use their paws. The kangaroos stand upright, balanced on their strong tail, and use their forepaws (small clawed paws, not human hands) to hold, lift and point. Kangaroos sit upright on the sofa with the tail curled alongside.
- **Scale:** Tommy is child-sized, Rangasamy a small adult size. The kangaroos are clearly taller, and Mullai is about twice Tommy's height.
- **Uncanny-valley fix:** simple fitted clothing, medium shots for spoken lines.
- **9:16:** faces in the upper two thirds, bottom fifth and right edge clear for the Shorts buttons. The 6-angle body sheets stay 16:9 because they are reference only.
