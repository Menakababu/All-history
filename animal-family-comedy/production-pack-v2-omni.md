# "பொண்ணு பாக்குற படலம்" – Animal Family Comedy
## Production pack v2 for Google Flow (Character section + Omni model)

**Locked from your brief:** 4 characters, 3 scenes, 3 scene images, 3 videos.
- Family 1 (groom's side): father + son (dogs)
- Family 2 (bride's side): mother + daughter (elephants)
- Animals in human costume, human activities, real animal bodies.

Every prompt block below starts with:
- **PROMPT TYPE** – IMAGE or VIDEO
- **ATTACH INGREDIENTS** – exactly what to attach (Omni allows 6–7)
- **SAVE AS** – the file name to rename the output to

**Shared style (already inside every prompt):** Stylized 3D animated feature-film look, Pixar-quality, expressive anthropomorphic animal characters who walk upright and behave like humans, true animal heads, fur or skin, snout or trunk, ears, tail, wearing traditional Tamil Nadu clothes, soft cinematic lighting, 16:9.
