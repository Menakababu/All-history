**Agent mode:** paste Step 1, then every Step 2 block, then every Step 3 block (or a few at a time). The agent attaches the saved characters, environment and images by the names shown and names every file itself. You do not name or organise anything.

**Characters:** use your saved Flow characters BY NAME (they carry look and voice): Kottaimuthu, Kokkayi, Pechiammavum, Kuppusamy, Poni, Mayandi Thatha, Ganesha. No `.png` files and no character sheets are needed.

**Video length in Flow:** 4 s and 6 s slots are generated as **8 s** clips (84 videos); 8 s slots are generated as **10 s** clips (8 videos). All action and speech finish inside the slot (first part of the clip); the rest is a calm hold you trim in the edit. If your Flow only offers 4/6/8 s, make the 8 s slots 8 s clips; they still fit.

**Name rule:** the hidden maker **யந்திரசாமி (Yanthirasamy)** is only spoken/sung in scenes 24, 56. He must NEVER appear on screen: no figure, face, hand, shadow or silhouette. Props that are not characters: tiny brass clockwork beetles, the oil-free clay lamp, a copper spiral token, an old cloth map.
