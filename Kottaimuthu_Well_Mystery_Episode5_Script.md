**Rules followed:** max 2 characters per scene · mixed 4s / 6s / 8s scene lengths · **YANTHIRASAMY (யந்திரசாமி) stays hidden**: he is only *named* exactly 3 times (Scenes 08, 11, 18) and is **never shown**, not even as a silhouette, a shadow, a hand or a voice. Only clues appear on screen.

**Existing characters used:** Kottaimuthu, Kokkayi, Pechiammavum, Kuppusamy, Poni, Mayandi Thatha, Ganesha (same approved character sheets as Episode 4, no new characters)

**Continuity from Episode 4:** the well is full, the giant wet handprint and the tiny clay lamp were left on the rim, the spiral mark is a clue, the coconut spins when Yanthirasamy is near, Ganesha trumpeted at two glints of light in the forest, and Mayandi Thatha said "நல்லவனா இல்லையான்னு… அடுத்த முறை தெரியும்." Episode 5 opens the morning after that cliffhanger.
**Time of day:** Scenes 1-4 morning · 5-6 day · 7-8 afternoon · 9-12 afternoon at the boulder · 13 dusk · 14-18 night at the well · 19-20 next dawn.
**New clues this episode (no face, no body):** a soft "டக்… டக்…" ticking from the well, a clay lamp that burns without oil, gear-like lines inside the old handprint, a copper spiral plate under the water tank, an old cloth water-map, the spiral on the boulder that glows near the lamp, fireflies flying in a spiral, a small clay pot that floats up with a leaf message, and a crack of blue-green light in the boulder.

## SCENES

### SCENE 01 — 4 sec — HOOK
**Characters:** Kottaimuthu
**Action:** Morning at the full well. Kottaimuthu presses his ear to the stone rim. A faint, steady "டக்… டக்…" comes from deep inside, like a small machine. His eyes widen.
**Camera:** Medium side shot, slow push-in on his ear against the stone.
**KOTTAIMUTHU (whisper, puzzled):** "இது தண்ணி சத்தம் இல்ல… இயந்திரம் ஓடுது!"

### SCENE 02 — 6 sec
**Characters:** Kottaimuthu, Kokkayi
**Action:** Kokkayi walks up holding the tiny clay lamp from the rim. The flame is still lit, yet the lamp has no oil. Kottaimuthu stares at the flame, then at her.
**Camera:** Medium two-shot, then a close-up of the steady flame.
**KOKKAYI (amazed):** "இந்த விளக்குல எண்ணெயே இல்ல… ஆனாலும் எரியுது!"
**KOTTAIMUTHU:** "அப்போ இது சாதாரண விளக்கு இல்ல!"

### SCENE 03 — 4 sec
**Characters:** Kokkayi
**Action:** Kokkayi crouches by the giant handprint on the rim, now dry and pale. Inside the palm are fine gear-like lines. She traces them with a fingertip.
**Camera:** Extreme close-up on the palm lines, tilt up to her curious face.
**KOKKAYI (whisper):** "இந்த கை ரேகையில சக்கரம் மாதிரி கோடுகள்!"

### SCENE 04 — 6 sec
**Characters:** Poni, Ganesha
**Action:** Ganesha calmly dips his trunk in the full well and drinks. Poni pats his side, smiling. His ears are relaxed, not flared.
**Camera:** Medium two-shot at eye level, slow drift.
**PONI (happy, child voice):** "கணேசா இப்போ பயப்படல… அப்போ வந்தவர் நல்லவர்தானே?"

### SCENE 05 — 8 sec
**Characters:** Pechiammavum, Kuppusamy
**Action:** At the village tap, clear water pours strongly into Pechiammavum's brass pot. She is delighted. Kuppusamy folds his arms, glancing toward the forest, thoughtful.
**Camera:** Medium two-shot, slow push-in on the pouring tap, then on Kuppusamy's face.
**PECHIAMMAVUM (joyful):** "பாத்தியாடா தம்பி… குழாயில தண்ணி கொட்டுது!"
**KUPPUSAMY (thoughtful):** "சந்தோஷம்தான் அம்மா… ஆனா யார் தர்றாங்கன்னு தெரியணுமே!"
**PECHIAMMAVUM (warm):** "யார் கொடுத்தா என்னடா… பிள்ளைங்க தண்ணி குடிச்சா போதும்."

### SCENE 06 — 4 sec
**Characters:** Kuppusamy
**Action:** Kuppusamy kneels by the blue water tank, wipes mud from its base and finds a small copper plate with a spiral mark.
**Camera:** Close-up on his hand wiping the plate, tilt up to his surprised face.
**KUPPUSAMY (surprised):** "இங்கயும் அதே சுத்து குறி… தொட்டி அடியில!"

### SCENE 07 — 6 sec
**Characters:** Kokkayi, Mayandi Thatha
**Action:** Afternoon at Mayandi Thatha's house. Kokkayi shows him the glowing-flame lamp. The coconut in his hand turns slowly and gently, not wildly. He nods.
**Camera:** Medium two-shot, slow push-in on the lamp and the slowly turning coconut.
**KOKKAYI:** "தாத்தா, இந்த விளக்கு எண்ணெய் இல்லாமலே எரியுது."
**MAYANDI THATHA (calm, slow):** "எரியட்டும்… கிணறு பாதுகாப்பா இருக்குன்னு அர்த்தம்."

### SCENE 08 — 8 sec — NAME #1
**Characters:** Mayandi Thatha, Kokkayi
**Action:** Mayandi Thatha unrolls an old cloth map on the string cot. It shows only unreadable wavy lines and one spiral. Kokkayi leans over it, eyes bright. (Only the map is shown: no drawing of a person.)
**Camera:** Top-down shot of the cloth map, then a medium two-shot.
**MAYANDI THATHA (slow, quiet):** "இதோ பாரு… யந்திரசாமி வரைஞ்ச தண்ணி வரைபடம். ஊருக்கு அடியில தண்ணி போற பாதை."
**KOKKAYI (excited):** "அப்போ இந்த சுத்து குறி… வழி காட்டுற குறி!"

### SCENE 09 — 6 sec
**Characters:** Poni, Kottaimuthu
**Action:** On the forest trail, Poni brushes damp moss off the big boulder and finds a spiral carved in the stone. Kottaimuthu crouches beside her and touches it. It feels warm.
**Camera:** Close-up on the carved spiral, pull back to a medium two-shot.
**PONI (excited):** "அண்ணா, பாறையிலயும் அதே சுத்து குறி!"
**KOTTAIMUTHU:** "அப்போ இந்த பாறைக்கு அடியில வழி இருக்கு!"

### SCENE 10 — 4 sec
**Characters:** Ganesha
**Action:** Ganesha rests his trunk against the boulder. A low, deep hum rises from under the stone. His eyes are calm and curious.
**Camera:** Low-angle close shot on the trunk touching the stone, slow push-in.
**SFX:** Deep underground hum, soft elephant rumble. No dialogue.

### SCENE 11 — 8 sec — NAME #2
**Characters:** Kuppusamy, Kottaimuthu
**Action:** Kuppusamy braces his shoulder against the boulder and pushes hard. It does not move at all. He leans on it, breathing hard. Kottaimuthu tilts his head, amused.
**Camera:** Medium two-shot, slight low angle, then a comic close-up of Kuppusamy's puzzled face.
**KUPPUSAMY (straining):** "நகரு… நகரு!"
**KUPPUSAMY (panting):** "என் பலத்துக்கே அசையலையே… யந்திரசாமி கதவு போல இருக்கு!"
**KOTTAIMUTHU (comic):** "அண்ணே, இது பலத்தால திறக்கற கதவு இல்ல!"

### SCENE 12 — 4 sec
**Characters:** Kokkayi
**Action:** Kokkayi lifts the clay lamp near the boulder. The carved spiral glows a soft blue-green. She lowers the lamp and the glow fades.
**Camera:** Close-up on the lamp and the spiral, then on her amazed face.
**KOKKAYI (whisper):** "விளக்கை தூக்குனா… குறி ஒளிருது!"

### SCENE 13 — 6 sec
**Characters:** Pechiammavum, Kottaimuthu
**Action:** Dusk on the house veranda. Pechiammavum hands Kottaimuthu a banana-leaf food packet and a shawl, tucking it into his arms with a caring look.
**Camera:** Warm medium two-shot, gentle push-in.
**PECHIAMMAVUM (warm):** "தம்பி, இந்தா சோறு. இராத்திரி கிணத்துக்கிட்ட பத்திரமா இரு."
**KOTTAIMUTHU (smiling):** "சரிம்மா, கவலைப்படாதீங்க!"

### SCENE 14 — 4 sec
**Characters:** Kokkayi
**Action:** Night at the well. Kokkayi sets the clay lamp on the stone rim. Its flame lights her determined face.
**Camera:** Close-up on the lamp, slow tilt up to her face.
**KOKKAYI (whisper):** "இன்னிக்கு ராத்திரி… யாரு வராங்கன்னு பார்ப்போம்."

### SCENE 15 — 6 sec
**Characters:** Kottaimuthu, Kokkayi
**Action:** Kottaimuthu and Kokkayi sit near the well. Fireflies gather above the water and drift in a slow spiral. Kokkayi puts a finger to her lips.
**Camera:** Medium two-shot, slow upward tilt following the fireflies.
**KOTTAIMUTHU (whisper):** "அக்கா, மின்மினி பூச்சிங்க சுத்து வடிவத்துல பறக்குது பாரு!"
**KOKKAYI (whisper):** "ஷ்ஷ்… அமைதியா இரு."

### SCENE 16 — 4 sec
**Characters:** Kottaimuthu
**Action:** The ticking "டக்… டக்…" returns from the well. Kottaimuthu freezes, lantern light shaking slightly, eyes wide and listening.
**Camera:** Tight close-up on his face, lantern flame flickering.
**KOTTAIMUTHU (whisper):** "டக்… டக்… திரும்ப வருது!"

### SCENE 17 — 8 sec
**Characters:** Kottaimuthu, Ganesha
**Action:** Rings spread across the well water. A small clay pot rises and floats gently to the rim carrying a folded leaf marked with a spiral and wavy lines (only water and light, no creature). Ganesha, curious and calm, lifts the leaf with his trunk and hands it to Kottaimuthu.
**Camera:** Wide shot of the rippling well, then a medium two-shot as Ganesha offers the leaf.
**KOTTAIMUTHU (amazed, whisper):** "கணேசா… தண்ணியில ஒரு சின்ன பானை மிதக்குது!"
**KOTTAIMUTHU (wonder):** "அது நமக்கு வந்த கடிதம் மாதிரி இருக்கே!"

### SCENE 18 — 4 sec — NAME #3
**Characters:** Kokkayi
**Action:** Kokkayi unfolds the leaf by lamplight. It shows a spiral, wavy lines and a boulder shape (unreadable marks only). She looks up, determined.
**Camera:** Over-the-shoulder close-up on the leaf, then on her face.
**KOKKAYI (excited whisper):** "பாறை படம்… யந்திரசாமி கூப்பிடறாரு!"

### SCENE 19 — 6 sec
**Characters:** Mayandi Thatha, Kuppusamy
**Action:** Dawn at the well. Mayandi Thatha studies the leaf message, coconut still in hand. Kuppusamy stands beside him, arms crossed, puzzled.
**Camera:** Medium two-shot, soft dawn light, slow push-in on the leaf.
**MAYANDI THATHA (quiet):** "இலை வந்துடுச்சு… அவன் நம்மள நம்புறான்."
**KUPPUSAMY (puzzled):** "அப்போ ஏன் முகம் காட்டல தாத்தா?"

### SCENE 20 — 4 sec — CLIFFHANGER
**Characters:** Mayandi Thatha, Kottaimuthu
**Action:** At the boulder at dawn, a thin crack of blue-green light now runs down the carved spiral. Kottaimuthu steps back, amazed. Mayandi Thatha stares into the light, grim and serious. The coconut stops with a soft click.
**Camera:** Slow push-in on the glowing crack and Mayandi Thatha's face, hard cut to black.
**MAYANDI THATHA (grim):** "வாசல் திறந்துடுச்சு… இனி உள்ள போகாம வழி இல்ல."
**End card (add in edit):** "தொடரும்… / To be continued…"

Clues only: ticking sound, oil-free lamp, gear lines in the handprint, copper spiral plates, the cloth water-map, the glowing boulder spiral, fireflies in a spiral, the floating clay pot with a leaf message, and the crack of light in the boulder.
