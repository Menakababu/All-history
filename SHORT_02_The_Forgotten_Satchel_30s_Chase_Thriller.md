**Story in one line:** A young station tea-stall helper finds a bag left on a bench and, with six minutes before the evening train leaves, runs across the station to return it to the old man who forgot it.

**Moral:** *Doing the right thing when no one is watching can change someone else's tomorrow.* (Never written on screen inside the clips. If you want it visible, add it as one end-card line in your editor, or put it in the title and description.)

**Why the chase is believable (the logic):**
- The bag holds the old man's medicines and hospital papers for tomorrow's surgery. That is why a stranger would run.
- The train is waiting on the far platform. A guard has to check coaches and wave a green flag before it moves, so six minutes is realistic.
- The runner takes the real route: platform → footbridge → far platform, slowed by a crowd and a luggage trolley. He jogs at a pace a normal fit young man can keep up, then slows to avoid people.
- The handover happens at the coach window while the train is still standing. When the train starts to roll, he steps back. Nobody runs alongside a moving train.
