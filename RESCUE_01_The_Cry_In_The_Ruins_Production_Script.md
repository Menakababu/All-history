## NATURAL-HUMAN-ACTIVITY RULES (the anti-AI-slop contract)

Baked into every video prompt. Keep them if you edit anything.
1. **Trained, not superhuman:** Ryker moves quickly but under control: low centre of gravity, short steps on uneven ground, one hand always free for balance or to protect the baby's head. He does not leap wide gaps; he **steps over a narrow crack** and **ducks under** a leaning beam.
2. **Real rescue behaviour:** he stops, listens, calls nothing out (no voice), tests a surface with his boot before trusting it, shines the headlamp before moving a beam, lifts debris with his shoulder and forearm slowly, and checks the baby's breathing before he moves.
3. **The baby is treated gently and is never in graphic peril:** she is swaddled in a cream blanket with yellow stars (no text), her face is visible and calm, she coos and stirs slightly, she is held upright against his chest with his hand cupping the back of her head, and dust never settles on her face (he turns his body and covers her with the blanket's edge).
4. **Real physics and logic:** an aftershock starts as a low tremor, then dust falls, then small pieces of plaster, then ceiling panels; cracks widen gradually; the camera shakes mildly, not wildly. The collapse in S04 happens **at a safe distance behind him** (about 30 m) once he is outside, and the dust cloud rolls out and thins.
5. **No fighting, no weapons, no injuries.** Ryker carries a rescue rope, a headlamp and a small first-aid pouch only.
6. **One continuous camera move per clip.** No whip pans, no fast cuts inside a clip.
7. **Continuity props:** Ryker's headlamp is **on** from S01 to S03 and off in S04; the coil of rescue rope sits on his **left** hip; his helmet and vest get progressively dustier; the blanket is cream with yellow stars throughout; the building's damage is identical in every plate.
