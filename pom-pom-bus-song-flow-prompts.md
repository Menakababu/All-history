- **POM POM BUS** = a cheerful, living cartoon bus in rainbow colors with big friendly headlight-eyes and a smile, no driver needed. It honks "pom pom" with each refrain.

## Costume pieces for each stop (props the kids put on)

- **Squirrel stop:** fluffy squirrel-tail clip-ons and tiny brown ear headbands, guava baskets.
- **Rabbit stop:** long white bunny-ear headbands, cucumber baskets.
- **Cuckoo stop:** small black-and-red bird beak caps with feather wings on the arms.
- **Peacock stop:** shimmering blue-green peacock feather capes and tail fans.
- **Waterfall stop:** bright raincoats and rubber boots, rainbow ribbons.
- **Mountain stop:** explorer hats and little binoculars.

## Hooks (so kids go crazy)

1. **Pom Pom horn:** the bus honks "pom pom" on every refrain, kids pull the horn cord and everyone copies. Easy to shout along.
2. **Costume change magic:** at every stop a golden sparkle swirl changes the kids' costumes instantly.
3. **Gift giving:** the kids hand over guavas and cucumbers, so every animal stop has a cute "thank-you" reaction.
4. **Animals dance back:** the squirrel scampers, the rabbit hops, the cuckoo calls and the peacock fans out, all in time with the refrain moves.
5. **Rainbow finish:** the waterfall rainbow becomes a giant arch above the bus at the end.
6. **Bus transformation:** the bus's color changes at every stop to match the friend (green for squirrel, white for rabbit, blue for peacock).

## Flow-safety rules

- Start every prompt with: `Family-friendly, G-rated cheerful children's cartoon.`
- The bus is a living cartoon, with no driver and no danger. When it moves, the kids are seated with seat belts, waving from inside the windows. When it stops, the kids dance on the ground in the meadow.
- All animals are friendly. Waterfall and mountain scenes keep the kids on a safe flat viewing area with a wooden railing, never near an edge.
- No brand or studio names, no real people, no readable text.
- If Flow blocks a prompt, remove "bus moving" and use "bus parked" for dance scenes.

## Workflow in Flow

1. Make the character sheets (Step 1).
2. Generate each scene's start image with the sheets attached as references.
3. Image to Video with that image as the first frame and the matching video prompt.
4. Veo can't lip-sync to the song. Prompts say "no music, no speech" and mouths move as if singing. Dance timing is matched by counts, then you fine-tune in the editor.
5. Flow's native lengths are 4/6/8 s. For the 10 s clips, generate 8 s and extend, or slow it to about 80 %.
