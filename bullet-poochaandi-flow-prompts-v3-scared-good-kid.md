# Bullet Poochaandi v3 – 3D Animated, Flow-Safe, "Scared → Good Kid" Edition

Song: "Urulaikizhangu Chella Kutty" (Tamil, 3:16 = 196 s)
Cast: **Amma**, **Urulaikizhangu Chella Kutty** (potato kid), **Bullet Poochaandi** (your attached image = design reference)

## Story logic (matches the lyrics)

Every verse follows the same 3-beat arc:
1. **Refuse:** Amma asks for a chore, Kutty cheekily says "mudiyaadhu pongamma".
2. **Scared:** Amma says "Bullet Poochaandi vandhuduvaaru!", Poochaandi appears looking big, stern and mock-menacing, and Kutty is comically frightened: eyes huge, trembling like jelly, sprout wilts.
3. **Good kid:** Kutty pleads "venaam mummy", then does the chore at lightning speed and proudly says "mudichittein". He earns a gold star each verse. 8 verses = 8 stars.

## Flow-safety rules (follow in every prompt)

- Start every prompt with: `Family-friendly, G-rated cartoon comedy.`
- Show fear as **cartoon slapstick**: "comically startled", "eyes pop wide", "trembling like jelly", "hides behind Amma", "pulls hood over eyes". Do NOT use "terrified / horrified / screaming in fear / traumatized".
- Poochaandi **never touches, chases, grabs or hits the child**. He stays at a distance (window, doorway, mirror, street) and only poses, revs and stares with a dramatic over-the-top face. No weapons.
- No blood, no real brands or studio names, no real people, no readable text.
- The child is always fully clothed (bath scene = raincoat and boots).
- If Flow still blocks a prompt: replace "flames/fire" with "magical orange sparkles", or replace "stern menacing face" with "dramatic serious face", then retry.
- Upload your Poochaandi image once, and ask Flow to turn it into a **3D animated character**. Then reuse that 3D result as the reference everywhere.

## Hooks (so kids go crazy)

1. **First 3 seconds (S1):** the bike bursts out of a glowing portal straight at the camera in a wheelie with a stern face, thunder flash, then a slow dramatic head-turn to the camera.
2. **"Bullet Bounce" (Poochaandi's 3-move dance, same in every chorus):**
   - **REV, REV:** fists twist the handlebar twice, a lightning-flash on each rev.
   - **WHEELIE:** the front wheel pops up, the feather crown bounces.
   - **STARE + SLOW POINT:** he freezes and slowly points at the camera. Kids can copy this and shout "Poochaandi!".
3. **Kutty's "Jelly Shake":** every time Poochaandi points, Kutty freezes and shakes like jelly, sprout drooping. Kids will copy this.
4. **Gold-star counter:** each "c" clip ends with a gold star popping next to Kutty. After 8 verses he has 8 stars.
5. **Sprout meter:** sprout wilts when he is scared, stands up proud when he finishes.
6. **Payoff ending (S26):** Kutty is a good kid, Poochaandi gives a slow approving nod, tips his crown and drops a giant gold star, Kutty salutes.
7. **Thumbnail:** S3 or S9 (Poochaandi's dramatic pose over a jelly-shaking Kutty).
8. **Shorts teaser (15–20 s):** S1 + S3 + S9 + S24 + S26.

## Workflow in Flow

1. Make the 3 character sheets first (Step 1).
2. Generate each scene image with the character sheets attached as references.
3. Image to Video with that scene image as the first frame and the matching video prompt.
4. Veo cannot lip-sync to the song. Prompts say "no music, no speech" so the audio is clean. Mouths move as if speaking; add a lip-sync tool if you want exact sync.
5. Flow's native lengths are 4/6/8 s. For the "10 s" clips, generate 8 s and extend, or slow it to about 80 %.

# Editing tips

- Place clips using the times in each heading, then slide by ear.
- Add a thunder-hit/whoosh transition and a small zoom-in on each REV, REV.
- Show a small star counter in the corner (1 to 8) as each "c" clip finishes.
- If a block feels short, add B1–B4 or repeat a "b" clip with a different crop.
- Verses 5 and 6 share lyrics, so S14–S16 and S17–S19 use different locations on purpose.
