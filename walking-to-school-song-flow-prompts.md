## Costumes

- **School uniform for all kids:** crisp white shirt, navy shorts or pinafore, red tie, ID card on a ribbon, black shoes, backpack and a water bottle.
- **Cycle helmets** on the cyclists.
- **A bus-driver cap** on the buses' mascot driver costume (a toy cap on the dashboard).
- Small props for the dance moves: a pretend steering wheel, a pretend bell, a tiny flag.

## Hooks (so kids go crazy)

1. **Beep-beep nose tap:** a simple nose-tap on "beep, beep" that kids will copy and shout along.
2. **Ching-ching bell flick:** three thumb flicks, with a sparkle "ching" appearing each time.
3. **Bujji the puppy** pops into every clip (wags his tail to the beat, tries the moves, carries a tiny backpack).
4. **Vehicles dance too:** the buses bounce and honk "beep, beep" in time and the bicycles' bells sparkle on "ching".
5. **Morning sunrise glow** makes the whole video bright and happy.
6. **Big finale:** everyone cheers at the school gate while balloons and confetti fall.

## Flow-safety rules

- Start every prompt with: `Family-friendly, G-rated cheerful children's cartoon.`
- Road safety is shown clearly: the kids walk on a wide pavement/sidewalk, hold hands in a line, Amma walks right behind them, and they cross at a zebra crossing with a friendly crossing guard. Cyclists wear helmets and stay on a separate bike lane. Buses stay in the road lane.
- Dance scenes happen on a safe, wide pavement or the school playground, never in the road.
- No brand or studio names, no real people, no readable text (use plain colored shapes on signs).
- If Flow blocks a prompt, replace "road" with "tree-lined street" and keep the kids on the pavement.

## Workflow in Flow

1. Make the character sheets (Step 1).
2. Generate each scene's start image with the sheets attached as references.
3. Image to Video with that image as the first frame and the matching video prompt.
4. Veo can't lip-sync to the song. Prompts say "no music, no speech" and mouths move as if singing. The dance timing is matched by counts, then you fine-tune in the editor.
5. Flow's native lengths are 4/6/8 s. For the 10 s clips, generate 8 s and extend, or slow it to about 80 %.
