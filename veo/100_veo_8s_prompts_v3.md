Each block is one complete, paste-ready prompt (look + single flowing action + sound + avoid list).

## 1. Donkey Stone Mill
```
Photorealistic vertical 9:16 documentary footage on a cinema camera, 35mm lens, shallow depth of field. One continuous 8-second take, no cuts, real-time speed, motion already underway in the first frame. Camera starts medium and slowly pushes in on a smooth dolly to end tight on the key detail. Natural on-location light, real skin and materials, correct physics, anatomically correct hands. Low side-angle tracking shot at donkey-shoulder height in a sunlit mud-brick mill yard at dawn. A weathered farmer in his 60s in a faded shirt and turban walks beside a blindfolded brown donkey, one palm resting on the long wooden pole that sweeps past the lens. The hooves thud into packed earth, kicking up small puffs of dust that glow in the low sun. The camera glides inward over the pole to the turning millstone as his free hand tips a scoop of wheat into its centre eye; the grains vanish, and in the final two seconds a soft curtain of pale flour spills continuously over the stone's rim into a cloth-lined trough, floating in a beam of light. Sound: only real ambient sound - hooves on earth, deep stone grinding, soft hiss of flour; no music, no voiceover. Avoid: text, captions, watermarks, music, voiceover, CGI or cartoon look, waxy skin, warped or extra fingers, morphing objects, glitching or jammed machinery, impossible physics, flicker, cuts, slow motion, speed ramps.
```
